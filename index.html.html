About Us

Kalpatharu Integrated Rural Development Society is a Karnataka-based non-profit focused on rural development and climate resilience.

Vision

To build resilient rural communities by empowering women and restoring ecosystems.

Mission

To promote sustainable livelihoods, climate-smart agriculture, and environmental restoration.
